Gonstead care

Your first visit in

Around 45 minutes to an hour, and you will leave knowing what is going on and what happens next.

What happens at a first Gonstead visit?

Around 45 minutes to an hour: your history, a full five-component analysis, imaging where appropriate, and usually your first adjustment.

The first visit is an assessment, not a quick adjustment. The Gonstead method is built on finding the exact segment causing the problem rather than treating the area that hurts, and that takes a proper look.

Why the first visit takes longer

A Gonstead analysis is deliberately slow at the start so that everything after it can be precise. Five separate lines of evidence have to agree before an adjustment is made, and gathering them properly is the difference between correcting a problem and chasing a symptom.

  • Wear something you can move comfortably in.
  • Bring any recent imaging or reports you already have.
  • Allow up to an hour so nothing is rushed.
