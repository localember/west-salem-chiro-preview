Gonstead care

Follow-up visits in

Shorter, focused appointments that keep your care moving in the right direction.

What happens at a follow-up visit?

A quick re-check with instrumentation and palpation, then a specific adjustment to whatever the re-check identifies. Much shorter than the first visit.

Follow-up visits are short and specific. We re-check the segments we are working on, adjust what needs adjusting, and leave the rest alone.

Why consistency does the work

Spinal problems rarely resolve on their own, and how quickly yours responds depends on how long it has been there, what you do all day, and how your body heals. Coming consistently early on is what makes the difference; the visits typically space out as things hold.

  • Book ahead to get the times that suit you.
  • Tell us about changes between visits, good or bad.
  • Keep going through the stretch where you feel better but are not finished.
